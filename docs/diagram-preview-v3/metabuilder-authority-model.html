MetaBuilder Authority Model

Question. Who submits the objective, who owns authority, who enforces it, and who may verify results?

Where we are. This view separates the requester from the principal. They are often the same human, but an agent requester remains a delegate unless independently granted principal authority.

Constructs and ownership
  • The principal owns meaning, success, effect ceilings, and reserved decisions.
  • The requester creates the ObjectiveRequest.
  • MetaBuilder admits effects and owns state transitions.
  • Workers produce claims; independent or controller verifiers produce verification records.
Reading the shapes
Stadium: actor, entry, or terminal outcome. Rectangle: callable or pure function. Cylinder: immutable typed value or durable record. Blue border: current or established. Green border: proposed or missing. Orange border: current but needing improvement. Yellow box: standing rule or evidence boundary.
Relationship convention

Solid arrows show a currently supported relationship. Dashed arrows show a proposed, weakly typed, indirect, or not-yet-supported relationship.

Plain-language glossary
  • Principal: the authority owner, not merely the message sender.
  • DelegationProof: evidence that a requester may act for the principal within stated bounds.
  • Attenuation: making a grant narrower, never broader.
Evidence boundary

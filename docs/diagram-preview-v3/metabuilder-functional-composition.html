MetaBuilder Functional Composition

Question. What typed values and functions prepare, compose, build, execute, and verify a harness?

Where we are. This is the primary function-composition view. Actors are intentionally absent; authority appears only as an immutable input value.

Constructs and ownership
  • Rectangles transform values.
  • Parallelograms are immutable typed values.
  • Subroutines are current MetaBuilder runtime functions.
  • The stadium gate is where the principal personally ratifies.
  • The controller turns claims into observations and proposed verification records.
Reading the shapes
Stadium: entry or terminal outcome. Rectangle: callable or pure function. Parallelogram: immutable typed value. Blue: current or established. Green: proposed or missing. Orange: current but needing improvement. Yellow: standing rule or evidence boundary.
Relationship convention

Solid arrows show a currently supported relationship. Dashed arrows show a proposed or not-yet-typed relationship.

Plain-language glossary
  • Callable phase: a contract plus the exact execution binding that implements it.
  • ResolutionLock: exact accepted contracts, adapters, policy, profile, and compiler identity.
  • Claim: a producer statement that is not yet controller evidence.
Evidence boundary

This projection does not show who owns authority, how roles authenticate, or the internal runtime state machine. Green callable bindings and typed schemas are proposed.

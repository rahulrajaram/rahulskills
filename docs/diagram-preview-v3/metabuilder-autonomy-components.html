Reading the shapes
Stadium: actor, entry, or terminal outcome. Rectangle: callable or pure function. Cylinder: immutable typed value or durable record. Blue border: current or established. Green border: proposed or missing. Orange border: current but needing improvement. Yellow box: standing rule or evidence boundary.
Relationship convention

Solid arrows show a currently supported relationship. Dashed arrows show a proposed, weakly typed, indirect, or not-yet-supported relationship.

Plain-language glossary
  • Composition-conformance kernel: a deterministic proof of typed resolution, execution, recovery, and replay—not autonomous coding.
  • Complete bounded harness: a later milestone with governed agents, isolated candidates, verification, finite continuation, and explicit stops.
Evidence boundary

Because this is a mixed overview, adjacency does not mean two nodes share the same construct type. It does not replace the functional, authority, or runtime projections and does not qualify a harness.
